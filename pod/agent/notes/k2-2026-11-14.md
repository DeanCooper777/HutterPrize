## What k2 is

`/workspace/sub/k2_src.tar.gz` = `st2_src.tar.gz` plus a rewrite of the two 16-bit-activation
kernels (files `cpp_infer/src/opt/act16.cpp`, `act16.h`, `arena_build.cpp`, `arena_build.h`,
`model_opt.cpp`, and a new test `test_act16.cpp`). Nothing else differs.

- The mlp.down and output-projection weights for the 16-bit path are now nibble-packed (96 B
  per column / row instead of 192) and sit in the main weight stream where the unused 8-bit
  arenas were. The 16-bit activation is split into its two bytes and the products use
  vpmaddubsw. AVX2 only, no new instruction sets.
- The int32 dot products are exactly the same, so the output must be BYTE-IDENTICAL to st2.
- Windows (Lunar Lake laptop, one pinned P-core, enwik6 with dictionary + transformer):
  compress 136.3 s -> 128.7 s (-5.6%), decompress 138.5 s -> 129.6 s (-6.4%), archives
  identical, round trip OK. The transformer alone is about 7% faster per token.
